Power is workload-dependent

A Gaming PC's preset wattage is not its constant consumption

The existing 500 W preset is illustrative. A Gaming PC's actual draw can vary substantially with its graphics card, processor, cooling, storage, power supply, game workload and GPU or CPU utilisation. A demanding game can produce a very different load from browsing, video playback or an idle desktop.

Monitor use, frame-rate settings, power limits, sleep behaviour and time spent idle also affect total electricity use. For a closer result, measure the complete setup with a suitable plug-in energy monitor and use your own tariff.

Practical guidance

What can change the estimate?

  • GPU and CPU utilisation can change draw during different games and applications.
  • A high-refresh or second monitor adds its own electricity use.
  • Idle, sleep and shutdown settings affect time spent drawing power.
  • Frame-rate caps and power limits can change gaming load.
  • Use a meter on the complete setup when you need measured rather than illustrative data.

Simple maths, made useful

How we calculate Gaming PC running cost

WattCost UK converts the entered power into kilowatts, multiplies it by usage hours and days, and applies the configured electricity price. Annual figures use the site's 52-week model and monthly figures divide the annual estimate by 12.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Gaming PC cost to run?

It depends on actual draw, workload, viewing time, idle time and your tariff. Use the calculator with measured power where possible.

Does a Gaming PC use 500 W all the time?

No. The 500 W preset is illustrative and is not a universal consumption figure. Actual draw changes with components and workload.

Does the monitor count?

The calculator estimates the power you enter. Include the monitor and other connected equipment in your measured setup if you want a whole-desk estimate.

Are four hours and five days a UK average?

No. They are clearly labelled editorial assumptions used to make the worked example easy to compare.
