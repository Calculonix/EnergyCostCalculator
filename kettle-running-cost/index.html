Quick answer

How much does it cost to boil a kettle?

It depends on the wattage, boiling time, amount of water and your electricity tariff. A 3,000 W kettle used for three minutes would use 0.15 kWh if it drew full power throughout; at the current WattCost UK reference tariff of the current rate, the cost is calculated by the existing engine. That is an illustrative example, not a measured laboratory result.

Heating only the water you need usually reduces energy use. Do not fill below the kettle’s stated minimum level, and use the calculator above with your kettle’s actual rating and routine.

Why cost changes

Water volume, boiling time and actual energy

A kettle's rated power describes the rate at which it can use electricity while heating. The energy used for one boil depends on how much water is heated, its starting temperature, the time the element runs and automatic shut-off. A 3,000 W kettle does not use 3 kWh every time it boils; three minutes at full power is 0.15 kWh.

Boiling only the required amount usually reduces the water that must be heated. A higher-wattage kettle can boil faster, but if two kettles heat the same water to the same temperature with similar efficiency, the total energy may be similar even though the power draw and duration differ.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to boil a kettle?

The cost depends on wattage, boiling time, water volume and tariff. Use the calculator with your own figures; a three-minute 3,000 W boil is only an illustrative starting point.

How much does a 3kW kettle cost to run?

It uses electricity at a 3 kW rate while drawing full power, but a boil uses only the fraction of a kWh represented by its runtime.

Does a higher-wattage kettle cost more?

It draws power faster but may boil sooner. Total cost depends on both power and time, not wattage alone.

Does filling the kettle fully cost more?

Usually, yes, because more water needs more energy to heat. Fill only what you need while staying above the minimum level.

Does a kettle use 3kWh every time it boils?

No. A 3 kW rating is power. A short boil uses a fraction of a kWh and varies with water, runtime and shut-off behaviour.
