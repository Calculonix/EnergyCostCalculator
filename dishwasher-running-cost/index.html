Rated power versus cycle energy

A dishwasher's rated power is not its whole-programme energy

A dishwasher can have a relatively high rated power because its heating element may draw a substantial load while heating water. That does not mean it continuously consumes that amount from the beginning to the end of a programme. Pumps, controls, pauses, heating stages and drying can all change the power profile.

For real running-cost planning, a tested kWh-per-cycle figure for the programme you use is generally more useful than multiplying the nameplate wattage by the full programme duration. The calculator provides a transparent equivalent-runtime estimate when you do not have that figure.

What changes the result

Programme, temperature, drying and load all matter

Longer or hotter programmes can require more heating, while drying settings can add to electricity use. Eco programmes may use a different combination of temperature, duration and water management. These effects vary by dishwasher and programme, so a generic wattage estimate should remain clearly labelled as illustrative.

  • Use a published kWh-per-cycle figure when the manufacturer provides one.
  • Compare the programme you actually use, not just the appliance's maximum rating.
  • Use your own tariff rather than treating the configured reference rate as universal.
  • Run suitable full loads so the energy is spread across more dishes.

Simple maths, made useful

How we calculate dishwasher running cost

WattCost UK converts the entered power into kilowatts, multiplies it by the active equivalent hours and usage frequency, and applies the configured electricity price. Annual figures use the site's 52-week model and monthly figures divide the annual estimate by 12.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does a dishwasher cost to run per cycle?

It depends on the programme, temperature, heating and drying stages, load, duration and tariff. A tested kWh-per-cycle figure is usually more useful than maximum wattage alone.

Does a dishwasher use its rated power throughout a programme?

No. Rated power describes a load at a point when the appliance is drawing it. The dishwasher can use less during pumps, controls, pauses and other stages.

What should I use if I know the energy per cycle?

Use the published kWh-per-cycle figure as your primary evidence where available. This calculator is useful for a transparent wattage and equivalent-runtime estimate when that figure is not available.

Do hotter programmes cost more?

They can require more energy for heating, but the exact result depends on the appliance, programme, load, drying and duration. Avoid treating any generic temperature comparison as a universal measurement.
