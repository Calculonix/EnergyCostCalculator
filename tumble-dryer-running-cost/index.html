Running costs explained

How much does a tumble dryer cost to run?

A tumble dryer’s running cost depends on its power rating, how long the programme runs, the load, temperature, moisture level and dryer type. A vented or condenser dryer may use a heating element for much of a cycle, while a heat-pump model can recover heat and often uses less electricity. The calculator uses your entered wattage and time, so it is an estimate rather than a substitute for the appliance’s measured consumption.

For the most useful result, check the rating plate or manual, note the energy use for a full programme where available, and enter your electricity price rather than relying on a generic UK average.

Per cycle

How much does a tumble dryer cost per cycle?

There is no universal cycle price. A short, lightly loaded sensor programme can use much less electricity than a long, hot timed programme. To estimate one cycle, enter the dryer’s power, set the hours per day to the cycle length, set days per week to 1, and read the daily result. The 2,500 W preset is a typical illustrative estimate, not a measurement of every tumble dryer.

Power vs energy

How much electricity does a tumble dryer use?

Watts measure power at a moment in time; kilowatt-hours measure energy used over a period. A 2,500 W dryer drawing its full rated power for one hour would use 2.5 kWh, but real dryers cycle their heating elements and may reduce power as clothes dry. Use the energy label or a plug-in monitor when you need a measured figure.

Dryer types

Heat-pump vs condenser vs vented tumble dryers

Heat-pump dryers reuse warm air in a closed system and are generally the most efficient type, although they can take longer and cost more to buy. Condenser dryers collect moisture inside the machine but usually still use a conventional heating element. Vented dryers exhaust warm, damp air outdoors. Model-specific energy labels are more reliable than assuming every dryer in a category uses the same amount.

Simple maths, made useful

How we calculate tumble dryer running costs

WattCost UK converts watts to kilowatts, multiplies by hours and days of use, and applies your electricity price. Annual results use the calculator’s 52-week model and monthly results divide the annual figure by 12. The same calculation engine powers the homepage and this page.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does a tumble dryer cost to run for one hour?

A typical dryer rated between 2,000 W and 3,000 W uses about 2 to 3 kWh if it draws its full rated power for one hour. Your tariff determines the price, and actual draw varies during a cycle.

How much does a tumble dryer cost per cycle?

It depends on the cycle length, load, temperature and dryer type. Enter your own wattage and cycle duration above for an estimate.

Is a heat-pump tumble dryer cheaper to run?

Usually, yes. Heat-pump dryers generally use less electricity because they reuse heat, but actual savings depend on the programme and model.

How much electricity does a tumble dryer use?

Usage depends on power, time, load and efficiency. A full-cycle kWh figure or plug-in monitor is more precise than rated wattage alone.

Does a tumble dryer use more electricity than a washing machine?

Often it does per cycle because drying requires extended heating, although the exact comparison depends on both programmes and appliances.
