Simple maths, made useful

How we calculate this

We convert the appliance's power into kilowatt-hours, multiply it by your usage and then apply your electricity price. Annual figures use the site's 52-week model, and monthly costs divide that annual figure by 12. The default price comes from Ofgem's published average unit rate — you can replace it with your own tariff.
